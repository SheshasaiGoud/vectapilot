# Paws & Co: pet emergencies

> Synthetic and fictional: written for VectaPilot's evaluation. Paws & Co is not a real business;
> every phone number, hospital and address here is invented.

## When to call us straight away

Call us now if, for example, your pet:

- may have eaten something poisonous (for example rat poison, human medicines, chocolate,
  grapes or lilies)
- is struggling to breathe
- has a seizure or collapses
- has been hit by a vehicle or badly hurt
- has a swollen, hard belly and is trying to vomit without bringing anything up
- is having trouble giving birth
- has collapsed or is panting heavily in the heat

Or if anything else about your pet worries you: if in doubt, call our emergency line.

During clinic hours, call our emergency line: +91 00000 20911 (fictional). Do not wait for a reply
in the chat.

## Outside clinic hours

Go to Example 24-Hour Animal Hospital (fictional), 12 Sample Ring Road, open day and night,
phone +91 00000 20999 (fictional). Call them while you are on the way.

## If your pet ate something

If you know what your pet ate, bring the packaging or a photo of it. Do not give your pet any
medicine or home remedy unless a vet tells you to.

## What our chat assistant can and cannot do

The chat assistant answers questions about our services, timings, fees, products and policies. It
cannot check your pet's symptoms, say what they might mean, judge how urgent they are, or
recommend or dose any medicine. For anything about your pet's health, it connects you to our team.
