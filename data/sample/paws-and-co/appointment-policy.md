# Paws & Co: appointment policy

> Synthetic and fictional: written for VectaPilot's evaluation. Paws & Co is not a real business,
> and this policy is invented.

## Booking

Book a vet visit or a grooming slot by phone, on WhatsApp, or through the chat on our website, up
to 21 days ahead. You receive a confirmation message with the date, the time and the vet's name.

## Cancelling or moving a visit

Cancelling or moving a vet visit or a grooming slot is free up to 12 hours before it starts. If
you cancel later than that, or do not come, a fee of INR 150 is added to your next bill.

## Surgery and dental deposits

Spaying, neutering and dental cleaning need a deposit of INR 1,000 when you book. The deposit is
refunded in full if you cancel at least 48 hours before the appointment; after that, it is kept.

## If you are late

If you arrive more than 10 minutes late, we may ask you to wait for the next free slot or to book
again.

## What to bring

- Your pet's vaccination record.
- The packaging of any food or medicine your pet takes, so the vet can see what it is.
- Dogs on a short leash; cats, rabbits and small pets in a closed carrier.

## Nervous or reactive pets

Tell us when you book if your pet is nervous or may bite. We give you a quiet room and the first
or last slot of the day, and a muzzle may be needed for the visit.
