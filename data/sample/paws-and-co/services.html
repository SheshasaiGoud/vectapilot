Vet and grooming services

Synthetic and fictional: Paws & Co is not a real business, and this page was written for VectaPilot's evaluation. Prices are in our fee list.

Vet consultations

A check-up or illness visit with one of our vets. A consultation slot is 20 minutes. Book ahead; walk-ins are seen only when a vet is free.

Vaccinations

Vaccination visits for dogs, cats and rabbits. The vet checks your pet first and decides which vaccines are due. Please bring your pet's vaccination record.

Microchipping

A microchip with registration in the Example Pet Register (fictional), done during a normal visit.

Dental cleaning

Teeth cleaning under anaesthesia, by appointment only, after a pre-anaesthetic check with a vet. Your pet stays with us for the day.

Spaying and neutering

For cats and dogs, by appointment only, after a pre-surgery check. We give you written preparation instructions when you book. A deposit is needed to book (see our appointment policy).

Grooming

  • Bath and dry, full groom, and nail trims for dogs and cats, by appointment.
  • Walk-in nail trims on Saturday, 11:00 to 13:00.

Tests at the clinic

Basic blood and urine tests are done at the clinic. For X-rays and ultrasound we refer you to Example Animal Imaging (fictional), 3 km away.

What we do not offer

We do not offer boarding, pet sitting, dog training or house visits.
