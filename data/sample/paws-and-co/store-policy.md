# Paws & Co: store returns and delivery

> Synthetic and fictional: written for VectaPilot's evaluation. Paws & Co is not a real business,
> and this policy is invented.

## Returns

You can return unopened items within 14 days with the receipt, for a refund or an exchange.

Opened food, treats, litter and prescription diets cannot be returned. If an item was damaged when
you bought it, bring it back within 14 days and we replace it.

## Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days. Refunds above INR 3,000 are
approved by the store manager; we tell you the decision within 2 working days.

## Prescription diets

Prescription diets are sold only with an authorisation from a vet: one of ours, or a prescription
from your own vet that is less than 6 months old.

## Home delivery

We deliver within 5 km of the store. Delivery is free for orders above INR 999; below that it
costs INR 80. Orders placed before 14:00 arrive the same day.

## Stock

Stock changes every day. To check whether an item is in stock, call or message the store team.
