## Opening hours

Vet clinic: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 19:00, and Sunday, 10:00 to 13:00 (vaccinations and
check-ups only on Sunday).

Pet store and grooming: every day, 10:00 to 21:00.

The vet clinic is closed on national public holidays; the store stays open.

## Where to find us

Paws & Co, 7 Sample Road, Example Colony (a fictional address). Customer parking is in front of
the store, and there is a water bowl and a shaded waiting area outside.

## Which animals we see

Our vets see dogs, cats, rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters and pet birds. We do not treat reptiles,
farm animals or horses; for those, we can give you the name of a specialist vet.

## Pets in the store

Pets are welcome in the store on a short leash or in a carrier. Please keep them away from the
open food bins.

## Payment and pet insurance

We accept cash, UPI, and debit or credit cards. We do not bill pet insurers directly: you pay at
the clinic, and we give you an itemised invoice for your claim.
