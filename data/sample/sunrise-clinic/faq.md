## Payment and insurance

We accept cash, UPI, and debit or credit cards. We do not accept cheques.

We are a cashless partner of two insurers: Example Health Cover and Sample Care Insurance (both
fictional). With any other insurer, you pay at the clinic and claim the amount back from your
insurer; ask for an itemised bill at the front desk.

password-protected; the password is sent to your phone by SMS.

The front desk cannot explain what a report means. Please book a follow-up with your doctor.
