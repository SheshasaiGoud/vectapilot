Our services

Synthetic and fictional: Sunrise Clinic is not a real clinic, and this page was written for VectaPilot's evaluation. Prices are in our Fee Policy.

General consultation

A visit with a general physician for adults and children over 16. A consultation slot is 15 minutes. Book ahead, or walk in when a slot is free.

Children's health

Check-ups and illness visits with our paediatrician for children up to 16 years. A slot is 20 minutes. A parent or guardian must come with every child.

Skin, hair and nails

Consultations with our dermatologist on Tuesday and Thursday afternoons. Cosmetic treatments (peels, laser, fillers) are not offered.

Physiotherapy

Sessions of 45 minutes with a physiotherapist, Monday to Saturday. A doctor's referral is not needed for the first session.

Vaccinations

Vaccinations for adults and children, by appointment. The doctor decides which vaccines are due; please bring your vaccination record or card.

Blood tests and sample collection

Sample collection at the clinic from 7:30 to 11:00, Monday to Saturday, no appointment needed. Home sample collection is available within 5 km of the clinic for an extra fee.

Some tests need fasting. Our lab test price list says which ones. Follow the instructions your doctor gives you, and if you take regular medicines, ask your doctor before you fast.

Health check packages

  • Basic health check: consultation, blood count, blood sugar and urine test.
  • Comprehensive health check: the basic check plus lipid profile, thyroid profile, kidney and liver tests, ECG and a review with the doctor.

What we do not offer

Sunrise Clinic has no emergency department, no X-ray or scans, no surgery, no dental care and no pharmacy. Video consultations are not available yet.
