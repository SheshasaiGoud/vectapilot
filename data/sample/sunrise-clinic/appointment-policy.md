# Sunrise Clinic: appointment policy

> Synthetic and fictional: written for VectaPilot's evaluation. Sunrise Clinic is not a real
> clinic, and this policy is invented.

## Booking an appointment

Book by phone, at the front desk, or through the chat on our website. You can book up to 30 days
ahead. You receive an SMS with your booking number and the doctor's name.

Walk-ins are welcome for a general consultation when a slot is free; patients with a booking are
seen first.

## Cancelling or moving an appointment

Cancelling or moving an appointment is free up to 24 hours before it starts. You can move an
appointment as often as you like within that window.

If you cancel less than 24 hours before the appointment, or do not come, a fee of INR 200 is
added to your next visit's bill.

## If you are late

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may ask you to wait for the next free slot or to book
a new appointment.

## What to bring

- A photo ID (Aadhaar card, passport or driving licence).
- Your previous prescriptions and test reports, if you have them.
- Your insurance card, if you want to use cashless insurance.

## Children

Patients under 18 years must come with a parent or a legal guardian, who signs the consent form.

## Reasons for the visit

When you book, we only ask which doctor or service you need. Please do not send symptoms or
reports through the chat; the doctor will ask about them during the consultation.
