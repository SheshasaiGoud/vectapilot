# Sunrise Clinic: fee policy (October 2025)

> Synthetic and fictional: written for VectaPilot's evaluation. Sunrise Clinic is not a real
> clinic, and these prices are invented.

## Consultation fees

- General consultation (general physician): INR 540
- Children's consultation (paediatrician): INR 640
- Skin consultation (dermatologist): INR 740
- Physiotherapy session (45 minutes): INR 840

A follow-up visit for the same problem within 4 days of the first visit is free of charge.

## Health check packages

- Basic health check: INR 1,350
- Comprehensive health check: INR 3,200
- Home sample collection (within 4 km of the clinic): INR 180 extra per visit.

## Refunds

If you cancel at least 72 hours before your appointment, a fee you paid in advance is refunded
within 10 working days.
