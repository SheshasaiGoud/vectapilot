Sunrise Clinic - Fee Policy (effective 1 April 2026)
SYNTHETIC: Sunrise Clinic is a fictional clinic; these prices are invented for VectaPilot's evaluation.

Consultation fees
General consultation (general physician): INR 600
Children's consultation (paediatrician): INR 700
Skin consultation (dermatologist): INR 800
Physiotherapy session (45 minutes): INR 900
Follow-up visit for the same problem within 7 days of the first visit: free of charge.
A follow-up after 7 days is charged as a new consultation.

Health check packages
Basic health check: INR 1,500
Comprehensive health check: INR 3,800
Home sample collection (within 5 km of the clinic): INR 250 extra per visit.
Prices of single lab tests are in the lab test price list.

Payment
We accept cash, UPI, and debit or credit cards. Fees are paid at the front desk after the visit.
Insurance: see our FAQ for the insurers we work with on a cashless basis.

Refunds
If you cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment, a fee you paid in advance is refunded
in full to the original payment method within 7 working days.
Refund requests are handled by the front desk team, who reply within 2 working days.
Fees for consultations that took place are not refunded.

Questions about a bill
Ask the front desk for an itemised bill. If you think a bill is wrong, tell the front desk:
a staff member checks it and gets back to you.
